Made for looking around

A familiar library.
A different kind of view.

Bring the albums you already have into a spacious viewer, with everyday controls close at hand.

01 / SPATIAL

Photos with depth

Eligible ordinary photos can open as spatial scenes prepared on your device. Existing spatial photos can be viewed in stereo.

02 / YOUR PACE

Two views, side by side

Open separate slideshow windows and arrange two together. Each slideshow keeps the order it opened with.

03 / EVERYDAY

Photos and videos

Browse albums, search names, filter photos or videos, and set a video to loop in the viewer.

04 / ORGANIZED

Albums that make sense

Move items between albums, find unfiled media, and use Recycle Bin to set items aside before permanent deletion.

05 / ON DEVICE

Your Photos stay yours

Gallery works with Apple Photos. Scene preparation happens on your device and does not create a permanent converted copy.

06 / HONEST

Always a way back

Some images cannot be prepared as spatial scenes. They remain available in 2D, so you can keep browsing.
