# Sharing photos and videos

To add a photo or video to Simple Vision Gallery, use the source app's Share button, choose **Save to Simple Vision Gallery**, then choose **Save to Photos**. The extension saves a copy of the file representation supplied by the source app. It does not decode or recompress that file, and it does not change the source item.

This uses the system Share extension. It does not try to launch or open the Gallery app after saving.

Photos may ask for permission to add items the first time you save. If access is denied, allow adding photos in Settings and try again. Cancel closes the share sheet without saving.

The Gallery's existing **Share** actions remain available for Photos items in the grid selection menu and in the viewer. They export the original photo or video resources through the system share sheet.

Only supported photo and video items can be saved. Unsupported items or provider errors show a message; the source stays unchanged.
