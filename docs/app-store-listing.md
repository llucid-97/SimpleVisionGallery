## Description

Simple Vision Gallery brings your Photos albums into visionOS. Open photos in separate spatial slideshow windows, then arrange two slideshows side by side. Videos also play in the viewer.

Spatial viewing is on by default. Eligible ordinary photos open as generated 3D spatial scenes. Existing spatial photos open in spatial stereo. Some photos cannot be generated as spatial scenes because of their size, shape, or image data; they remain available in 2D. Spatial scenes are created for viewing and are not saved as permanent conversions in Photos.

Watch videos in the same viewer and turn on looping when you want playback to repeat. Move photos and videos between albums. The viewer’s Recycle Bin action moves an item straight into an ordinary Photos album named “recycle bin”; it does not delete that item from your Photos library. Grid removal also moves selected items to the recycle-bin album. Inside that album, you can permanently delete selected items with confirmation.

Rename your albums, find them even with a small typo, and quickly reuse your three most recent move destinations. Select all items in an album or choose a range.
