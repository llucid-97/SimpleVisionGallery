# App Store listing draft

## Name

Simple Vision Gallery

## Subtitle

Your Photos in spatial scenes

## Promotional text

Open supported photos as spatial scenes, browse videos, and arrange two slideshows side by side.

## Description

Simple Vision Gallery brings your Photos albums into visionOS. Open photos in separate spatial slideshow windows, then arrange two slideshows side by side. Videos also play in the viewer.

Spatial viewing is on by default. Eligible ordinary photos open as generated 3D spatial scenes. Existing spatial photos open in spatial stereo. Some photos cannot be generated as spatial scenes because of their size, shape, or image data; they remain available in 2D. Spatial scenes are created for viewing and are not saved as permanent conversions in Photos.

Watch videos in the same viewer and turn on looping when you want playback to repeat. Move photos and videos between albums. The viewer’s Recycle Bin action moves an item straight into an ordinary Photos album named “recycle bin”; it does not delete that item from your Photos library. The grid also has a separate Delete from Library action for selected items. Gallery and Photos each show a confirmation before that deletion.
